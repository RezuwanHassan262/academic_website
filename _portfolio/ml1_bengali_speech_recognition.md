Built a regional speech corpus covering six major Bengali dialect regions (Chittagong, Sylhet, Rangpur, Rajshahi, Noakhali, Barishal) and fine-tuned **Wav2Vec2** for Bengali dialect transcription. The dataset led to a national-level datathon (*ভাষা-বিচিত্রা* at IUT). Subsequently fine-tuned a **Whisper** model and deployed it on Hugging Face Spaces.

### Key Outcomes
- First large-scale annotated Bengali dialect speech corpus
- Wav2Vec2 and Whisper models trained on regional data
- Live ASR demo available on Hugging Face Spaces
- Resulted in the [RegSpeech12 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24096) and [AACL-IJCNLP 2025 paper](https://aclanthology.org/2025.ijcnlp-short.17/)
