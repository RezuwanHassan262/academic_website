Fine-tuned **YOLOv8n** on a custom dataset collected from video frames of Bangladeshi road scenes, auto-labeled with **Roboflow**. Detects vehicles, pedestrians, and signboards. Deployed as a Streamlit app on Hugging Face Spaces and also runs locally without API support.

Python · YOLOv8 · Dataset Creation · Roboflow · Hugging Face · Streamlit
