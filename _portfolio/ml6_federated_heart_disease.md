Implemented a **Horizontal Federated Random Forest** for heart disease detection from decentralized local hospital data. Secure model parameter sharing was performed across multiple hospital clients without exposing patient records.

### Key Results
- **7.1%** accuracy improvement for the federated central model
- **2%** and **6%** improvement for each respective client
- Privacy-preserving: no patient data leaves local nodes
