An end-to-end AI chatbot, **UnmadBot**, trained on a dataset built from the past magazine content of
*Unmad* — Bangladesh's long-running satire magazine — supervised by
[Prof. S M Taiabul Haque](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=tMCRaaEAAAAJ&hl=en).

- Designed an end-to-end AI chatbot, **UnmadBot**, trained on a dataset drawn from Unmad's past magazine content
- Fine-tuned **Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct** with LoRA on Bangla instruction-tuning and distillation data, deploying on **Azure ML / AKS** with containerized model serving
- Investigating how foundation models can understand and generate culturally grounded satire and humor
