Bengali.AI, together with the IUT Computer Society (IUTCS), ran a nationwide AI competition called **"ভাষা-বিচিত্রা" (Language Variety)** to find the next big talents in speech recognition for regional dialects.

Despite advances in Automatic Speech Recognition, our findings show clear limitations in zero-shot and fine-tuned performance on local dialects. This datathon targeted that gap by seeking solutions that improve ASR for diverse Bengali dialects — a language lacking extensive technological support for its regional variation.

Alongside my teammates from Bengali.AI, **Team Apocalypse** co-ran the workshop.
