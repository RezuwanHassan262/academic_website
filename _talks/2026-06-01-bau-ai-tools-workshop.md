Invited to Bangladesh Agricultural University (BAU), Mymensingh, to discuss the current state of Bangla in AI as part of a panel discussion.

I additionally served as a workshop instructor, demonstrating how AI can be productively leveraged to automate non-technical workflows and everyday tasks. It was an honour to share the stage with this prominent group of Bangla NLP researchers.

Special thanks to the [Bangladesh Agricultural University Language Club (BAULC)](https://www.linkedin.com/company/baulc20/) for the invitation and hospitality.
