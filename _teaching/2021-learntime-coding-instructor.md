LearnTime is a Rajshahi-based e-learning platform. This was a remote, part-time role of 3–4 hours a week, running from November 2021 to December 2022.

### Responsibilities
- Teach programming (in Python) to people of all ages — especially children — from non-programming backgrounds with little to no prior experience
- Write notes and course documents, and set problems for tests
- Provide worked solutions to the problems students struggle with
- Teach data analytics using Python to interested learners
