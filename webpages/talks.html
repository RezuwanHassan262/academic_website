Talks & Workshops

A Hands-on Guide to AI Tools for Non-Technical Workflows — Workshop Mentor

Panel Discussion & Workshop · Bangladesh Agricultural University (BAULC) · Mymensingh, Bangladesh
2026-06-01

Invited to Bangladesh Agricultural University (BAU), Mymensingh, to discuss the current state of Bangla in AI as part of a panel discussion.

ভাষা-বিচিত্রা — National AI Workshop on Speech Recognition — Workshop Host & Mentor

Workshop · Islamic University of Technology (IUTCS) · Gazipur, Bangladesh
2024-03-01

Bengali.AI, together with the IUT Computer Society (IUTCS), ran a nationwide AI competition called "ভাষা-বিচিত্রা" (Language Variety) to find the next big talents in speech recognition for regional dialects.

Despite advances in Automatic Speech Recognition, our findings show clear limitations in zero-shot and fine-tuned performance on local dialects. This datathon targeted that gap by seeking solutions that improve ASR for diverse Bengali dialects — a language lacking extensive technological support for its regional variation.

Alongside my teammates from Bengali.AI, Team Apocalypse co-ran the workshop.

NLP Datathon Bootcamp — Workshop Mentor

Workshop · United International University (UIUCCL) · Dhaka, Bangladesh
2024-02-01

Ran a workshop on behalf of Bengali.AI, together with teammates, for the bootcamp of the inter-university Natural Language Processing competition organised by UIUCCL.

AI Research & Academic Development Webinar

Invited Talk (Webinar) · BRAC University Computer Club (BUCC) · Dhaka, Bangladesh (Online)
2023-12-01

Invited to speak at a webinar organised by the BRAC University Computer Club on AI, Bangla NLP, and the importance of doing impactful research.

A large part of the session was practical advice for undergraduates: how to pick a thesis topic that is both tractable and worth doing, how to read a research area before committing to it, and how to turn a thesis into a publishable contribution.

Talk with Researchers — Bengali Language in NLP

Invited Talk (Webinar) · Andromeda Space & Robotics Research Organization (CUET) · Chattogram, Bangladesh (Online)
2023-08-01

Invited to speak at a webinar on AI and Bangla NLP organised by the Andromeda Space & Robotics Research Organization from CUET.

Topics included how Bengali phonology and morphology complicate speech and text modelling, and how students can get started contributing to Bangla NLP research.

Open-Source Linguistic Initiatives for Bangla — Ministry of Foreign Affairs

Invited Talk · Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Bangladesh · Dhaka, Bangladesh
2023-06-01

Invited to talk about multiple open-source linguistic project initiatives and to represent Bengali.AI to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Bangladesh.

The talk framed Bangla NLP as national digital infrastructure — covering the speech, dialect and IPA-transcription corpora being built in the open, and why publicly released resources matter for a language still treated as low-resource by mainstream foundation models.

AI Symposium — Exploring Bangla NLP with Bengali.AI

Invited Talk · Tech Topia · Bangladesh (Online)
2023-05-01

Invited as a speaker for a symposium on Bangla NLP organised by Tech Topia.

The session covered the state of Bengali as a low-resource language, the open-source datasets and tooling Bengali.AI has released to close that gap, and where new contributors can plug into ongoing Bangla speech and text projects.
