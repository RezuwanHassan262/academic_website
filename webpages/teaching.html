Natural Language Processing (NLP) for Beginners using Python

Online Course (Instructor & Course Designer) Lead Academy · Dhaka, Bangladesh

Lead Academy approached me to design and deliver an NLP course by developing the pre-recorded content for it, and I accepted the offer. I worked as an instructor there on a contractual basis.

The course — "Natural Language Processing (NLP) for Beginners using Python" — covers everything one might need to get started with NLP, from the very basics through to training deep learning models and using existing large language models.

Coverage

  • Text preprocessing, tokenisation, and classical NLP pipelines
  • Vector representations and statistical language modelling
  • Deep learning approaches to NLP tasks
  • Working with pre-trained large language models
  • Hands-on Python demonstrations for every concept, plus walkthroughs of the surrounding tooling and platforms

Rather than staying theoretical, each topic is demonstrated practically in Python alongside the relevant tools and platforms.

A 2-Hour AI Workshop on Language and AI Tool Usage, Academic Thinking & Smarter Learning Tech — Workshop Mentor

Panel Discussion & Workshop · University of Dhaka · Dhaka, Bangladesh
2026-08-20

I had the opportunity to speak alongside Abdullah Ibne Hanif Arean and Mirza Nihal Baig at “AI Beyond Answers,” organized by the Dhaka University IT Society (DUITS), where I delivered a session titled “CUTTING THROUGH THE NOISE: A Rigorous Guide to AI Learning Resources That Actually Build Understanding.”

With AI tools and learning resources growing rapidly, knowing how to use them effectively is becoming just as important as knowing that they exist. I discussed practical ways to leverage AI tools in everyday and non-technical workflows, along with where Bangla currently stands in the AI landscape and how people can make better use of AI in their native language. ভাষা-বিচিত্রা — National AI Workshop on Speech Recognition — Workshop Host & Mentor

Workshop · Islamic University of Technology (IUTCS) · Gazipur, Bangladesh
2024-03-01

Bengali.AI, together with the IUT Computer Society (IUTCS), ran a nationwide AI competition called "ভাষা-বিচিত্রা" (Language Variety) to find the next big talents in speech recognition for regional dialects.

Despite advances in Automatic Speech Recognition, our findings show clear limitations in zero-shot and fine-tuned performance on local dialects. This datathon targeted that gap by seeking solutions that improve ASR for diverse Bengali dialects — a language lacking extensive technological support for its regional variation.

Alongside my teammates from Bengali.AI, Team Apocalypse co-ran the workshop.
